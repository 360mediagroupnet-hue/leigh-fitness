    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    What happens in the first session?

    It's a chat, not a test. You talk through where you're at and what you want, fill in a short intake, and Leigh builds your plan around it.

    I haven't trained in years. Is this for me?

    Yes. A lot of Leigh's clients are starting late or coming back after a long break. The questionnaire means your plan starts where you are.

    Do I need a Goodlife membership?

    Only if you want to train at the gym. With home visits, you don't need a membership at all.

    Where do home visits cover?

    Maroochydore and the suburbs around it. Tell Leigh where you are and he'll let you know.

    What if I have an old injury?

    Put it in the questionnaire and mention it on your first chat. Your plan works around it. If it needs a physio or doctor first, Leigh will tell you.

    Do you help with food?

    Yes. Nutrition advice is part of every program, because training on its own rarely gets the result.

    I just want a program to run myself. Can you do that?

    That's Self-Led. Leigh builds your program, trains you at the start, middle and end, and checks in each month while you run it.

    What's the difference between the three?

    How much Leigh does. All In is everything: two sessions a week, your training and eating plans, and weekly check-ins. Coached is about half that. Self-Led is about a quarter.

    Can I just book single sessions?

    Yes. A one-hour session is $90 for just you, or $140 for a couple. A good way to start before you commit to a program.
