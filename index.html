Leigh. 23+ years of results.

Leigh's coaching career started in Long Beach, California in 2001, training at By My Side Personal Training Studio in Hollywood and Gold's Gym Redondo Beach. Back home, he started his personal training business in October 2001 at Fitness First North Quay, Brisbane.

Most personal trainers last two or three years. Staying in this game for over two decades comes down to one thing: getting clients results and looking after them along the way. Train with Leigh and you get all of that experience, plus constant support and direction until your goals are reached.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a gym membership?

No. You don't need to be a member of a health club, although having access to one can help your goals. Leigh will work with what you've got.

I'm a complete beginner. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Leigh trains beginners right through to experienced lifters, and builds your plan around where you are now.

Do you help with nutrition?

Yes. Training, programming and nutrition advice all come together. Results like Harry's come from fixing both.

Where are you based?

What hours do you train?
